# NYAYAI – Digital Evidence AI
## Project Overview & Mission Specification

---

### 1. Executive Summary

**NYAYAI** is an enterprise-grade, forensically sound Digital Evidence AI platform engineered to assist legal professionals, forensic investigators, law enforcement agencies, and the judiciary. The platform ingests, verifies, analyzes, correlates, and documents digital evidence while enforcing mathematical proof of evidence integrity and maintaining an unbroken cryptographic chain of custody.

In legal proceedings, digital artifacts (images, video, audio, documents, chat logs, call detail records) are easily challenged on grounds of tampering, spoliation, improper handling, or lack of provenance. NYAYAI bridges the gap between state-of-the-art computational forensics, explainable artificial intelligence, and judicial admissibility standards under the **Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 (BSA)** (formerly Section 65B of the Indian Evidence Act, 1872) and **ISO/IEC 27037**.

         ▼

### 3. Legal & Regulatory Compliance Framework

NYAYAI is constructed strictly around legal admissibility standards:

1. **Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA), 2023 (Section 63 & 65B equivalents)**:
   - Verifiable certification that the electronic record was produced by a computer in regular use.
   - Proof that the integrity of the contents has remained uncorrupted throughout custody.
   - Generation of mandatory electronic evidence certificates signed by competent authorities.
2. **ISO/IEC 27037:2012**:
   - Adherence to standard procedures for the identification, collection, acquisition, and preservation of digital evidence.
3. **Federal Rules of Evidence (FRE) Rule 902(13) & (14) Compatibility**:
   - Certified records generated by an electronic process or system that produces an accurate result, verified by cryptographic hash values.

### 4. Core Security Principles

- **Zero-Mutation Policy (WORM)**: Original uploaded files are placed into a read-only secure vault immediately upon intake. No analysis algorithm or pipeline step is ever permitted to overwrite, re-encode, or modify the original binary file.
- **Cryptographic Chaining**: Every custody action produces an immutable block linking the current state to the previous block's SHA-256 hash.
- **Auditable Provenance**: Every access, analysis run, view, export, or status change is recorded with actor ID, timestamp, and signature.
- **Zero Hallucination / Evidence-Grounded AI**: The platform forbids ungrounded AI confidence claims. AI models output probabilistic estimates with documented limitations and explicit confidence intervals.
